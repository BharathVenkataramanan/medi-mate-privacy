Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

01Information You Provide

MediMate allows users to enter and manage information including:

  • Medication names, dosages, and schedules.
  • Medication reminder preferences.
  • Medication adherence records, including taken and missed doses.
  • Names or identifiers associated with medication profiles, including family profiles.

This information is provided voluntarily to enable the application's medication management features.

02How Your Information Is Used

Information entered into MediMate is used to:

  • Display and organise medications.
  • Generate medication schedules and reminders.
  • Maintain medication adherence records.
  • Check for potential interactions between medications.
  • Manage separate medication profiles.
  • Display relevant medication information and safety alerts.

MediMate does not use medication information for targeted advertising or sell users' personal health information.

03Data Storage

MediMate's core medication management features are designed to store medication records locally on the user's device.

Information stored locally remains under the user's control and may be affected by the device's operating system, backup settings, and security configuration.

Users are encouraged to protect their devices using appropriate security measures, including device passcodes and software updates.

04Notifications

With your permission, MediMate may send notifications reminding you to take scheduled medications.

Depending on your device settings, notifications may contain medication names and scheduled times and may be visible on your lock screen.

Notification permissions can be modified or withdrawn at any time through your device's settings.

05Data Sharing

MediMate does not sell personal medication records to advertisers or data brokers.

Users may choose to export or share medication records using available application features. Any information deliberately shared or exported by the user may become subject to the privacy practices of the receiving application or service.

06Data Retention and Deletion

Medication records stored locally remain on the device until they are deleted through available application functions or removed along with the application's locally stored data.

Deleting the application will generally remove its local application data. Copies may remain in device backups according to the user's backup settings.

Users may contact us regarding privacy concerns or requests concerning information under our control.

07Data Security

We recognise the sensitivity of health-related information and seek to minimise unnecessary collection and transmission of personal information.

However, no electronic storage system or method of data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08Children's Privacy

MediMate is intended for individuals managing medications for themselves or others under their care.

Where information concerning children is entered, it should be managed by a parent, guardian, or other authorised caregiver.

MediMate is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate authorisation.

09Medical Information Disclaimer

MediMate is intended to support medication organisation and awareness. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions regarding medication use, dosage adjustments, or potential drug interactions.

10Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in application functionality, data handling practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
